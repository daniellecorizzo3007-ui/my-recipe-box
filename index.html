Ultimate Bakery-Style Biscoff Lava Cookies

A thick bakery-style brown butter cookie with a molten Biscoff cheesecake lava centre inspired by Biscoff truffles and finished with a silky Biscoff white chocolate drizzle and crushed Biscoff biscuits.

Yield: 12 cookiesPrep: 45 minBake: 12-14 minTotal: approx. 24 hrs

Key: 🕒 Can Be Prepared In Advance | 🥶 Refrigerate | ❄️ Freeze | 🔥 Reheat Before Use | 💡 Professional Baker's Tip

Serving Timelines

Fastest Serve Method: Approximately 30 minutes if all advance preparation is complete.

Same Day Method: Approximately 8 hours.

Ultimate Flavour Method: Approximately 24 hours including overnight dough chilling.

Advance Preparation Steps

  • Step A1 - Brown Butter: Prepare up to 7 days in advance. Refrigerate or freeze.
  • Step A2 - Centres: Prepare your chosen centre up to 48 hours ahead and freeze until solid.
  • Step A3 - Stuffed Cookie Dough Balls: Prepare and freeze up to 3 months in advance.
  • Step A4 - White Chocolate Biscoff Drizzle: Prepare up to 72 hours in advance and gently reheat before use.

Full Method

  1. Prepare brown butter. Place the butter into a medium saucepan over medium heat. Melt completely, then continue cooking while stirring frequently until foaming slows, golden brown specks appear and a nutty caramel-like aroma develops. Immediately remove from heat and pour into a heatproof bowl, including all browned milk solids. Weigh and adjust to 210g.
  2. Make cookie dough. Beat browned butter, white sugar and brown sugar for 2-3 minutes. Add egg, yolk and vanilla and mix until smooth.
  3. Dry ingredients. Whisk flour, cornstarch, baking soda, baking powder, salt, milk powder and cinnamon. Add to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined. Fold through white chocolate.
  4. Chill dough. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
  5. Prepare the centres. Choose lava, truffle or plain Biscoff. Line a tray with baking paper. Portion the chosen centre into 12 pieces and freeze until solid.
  6. Stuff the cookies. Divide dough into 12 x 90-95g portions. Flatten, place one frozen centre inside, seal completely and roll into tall dough balls.
  7. Bake. Bake at 180°C fan or 190°C conventional until golden around the edges and slightly underbaked in the centre. Cool on tray for 15-20 minutes.
  8. Decorate. Melt Biscoff spread and white chocolate in 20-second intervals. Drizzle over cooled cookies, sprinkle with crushed Biscoff biscuits and allow to set for 5-10 minutes.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Warm dough and softened centres are the most common reason for leaking or misshapen cookies. Work with only 2-3 dough portions at a time and keep the rest chilled or frozen.

Notes

Reheating

Freshly baked: cool 15-20 minutes. Day 2 cookies: microwave 8-10 seconds. Refrigerated cookies: microwave 12-15 seconds. Frozen cookies: thaw overnight or microwave at reduced power.

Storage

Room temperature: up to 3 days. Refrigerator: up to 5 days. Freeze unbaked dough balls: up to 3 months.

Troubleshooting

Centre not molten? Filling was not frozen enough. Cookies spread too much? Dough was not chilled long enough. Cookies dry? They were overbaked.
