ULTIMATE BAKERY-STYLE BISCOFF LAVA COOKIES

A thick bakery-style brown butter cookie with a subtle caramel spice flavour, filled with a molten Biscoff cheesecake lava centre inspired by Biscoff truffles and finished with a silky Biscoff white chocolate drizzle and crushed Biscoff biscuits.

Preparation: 45 minutes|Cooking: 12–14 minutes|Chilling: Overnight recommended|Total: Approx. 24 hours|Yield: 12 cookies
KEY:🕒 Can Be Prepared In Advance🥶 Refrigerate❄️ Freeze🔥 Reheat Before Use💡 Professional Baker's Tip

SERVING TIMELINES

Fastest Serve Method: If all advance preparation has been completed, cookies can be baked and served in approximately 30 minutes.

Same Day Method: Allow approximately 8 hours from start of preparation until serving.

Ultimate Flavour Method: Allow approximately 24 hours including overnight dough chilling.

ADVANCE PREPARATION STEPS

STEP A1 – Brown Butter 🕒🥶

Preparation Time: Approx. 10 minutes OR can be prepared up to 7 days in advance.

  • Place 250g Unsalted Butter into a saucepan over medium heat.
  • Cook, stirring frequently, until golden brown specks appear and a nutty aroma develops.
  • Immediately transfer to a heatproof bowl, including all browned milk solids.
  • Weigh the browned butter; target yield is 210g.
  • If slightly over 210g, remove the excess; if slightly under, add 1–2 teaspoons of milk.
  • If preparing on the same day, cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for approximately 1 hour until solidified.

Storage: Refrigerate up to 7 days in an airtight container or freeze up to 3 months.

Before Use: Remove from refrigerator, cut into approximately 2cm cubes and use immediately.

STEP A2 – Lava Centres 🕒❄️

Preparation Time: Approx. 15 minutes plus freezing OR can be prepared up to 48 hours in advance.

  • Combine 180g Biscoff Spread, 90g Cream Cheese and 20g White Chocolate in a microwave-safe bowl.
  • Microwave for 20–30 seconds, then whisk until completely smooth.
  • Fold through 80g finely crushed Biscoff biscuits and a pinch of fine salt.
  • Freeze for 1 hour, then divide into 12 equal portions of approximately 30g each.
  • Roll gently into balls and freeze again until solid.

Storage: Refrigerate on a baking-paper-lined tray or plate, covered with plastic wrap, for up to 48 hours; freeze up to 3 months.

Before Use: Use directly from the refrigerator or freezer; frozen filling is essential for the lava effect.

STEP A3 – Stuffed Cookie Dough Balls 🕒❄️

Preparation Time: Approx. 15 minutes OR can be prepared up to 3 months in advance.

  • Divide chilled dough into 12 equal portions of approximately 90–95g each.
  • Flatten each portion slightly.
  • Place one frozen lava centre into the middle of each dough portion.
  • Wrap dough around the filling and seal completely.
  • Roll into tall dough balls.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Tall dough balls create thicker cookies with gooey centres.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: If any filling is exposed, patch with extra dough before freezing.

Storage: Freeze on a baking-paper-lined tray until firm, then transfer to an airtight container or freezer bag for up to 3 months.

Before Use: Bake directly from frozen using the frozen baking instructions in STEP 6.

STEP A4 – White Chocolate Biscoff Drizzle 🕒🥶🔥

Preparation Time: Approx. 5 minutes OR can be prepared up to 72 hours in advance.

  • Combine 120g Biscoff Spread and 80g White Chocolate in a microwave-safe bowl.
  • Microwave in 20-second intervals, stirring between each interval, until smooth.
  • Allow drizzle to cool completely to room temperature before storing.

Storage: Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator up to 72 hours or freeze up to 1 month.

Before Use: Microwave at 800W on HIGH for approximately 10 seconds OR until thick, melted and gooey; do not overheat or it may become thin and watery.

METHOD

STEP 1 – Prepare Brown Butter

  • If using pre-prepared brown butter, remove from refrigerator and cut into approximately 2cm cubes.
  • If preparing fresh brown butter, cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for approximately 1 hour until solidified.

STEP 2 – Make Cookie Dough

  • Beat together 210g Brown Butter, 100g White Sugar and 160g Brown Sugar for 2–3 minutes until slightly lighter in colour.
  • Add 1 Large Egg, 1 Egg Yolk and 10ml Vanilla Extract.
  • Mix until smooth and fully incorporated.

STEP 3 – Add Dry Ingredients

  • Whisk together 340g Plain Flour, 20g Cornstarch, ¾ tsp Baking Soda, ½ tsp Baking Powder, ¾ tsp Fine Salt, 10g Milk Powder and ¼ tsp Ground Cinnamon.
  • Add dry ingredients to the wet ingredients.
  • Mix only until no dry streaks remain.
  • Fold through 80g White Chocolate Chunks.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Do not overmix; overmixing develops gluten and can produce tougher cookies.

STEP 4 – Chill Dough

  • Cover the dough and refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours.
  • For best flavour and texture, chill overnight.
  • For the ultimate bakery-style result, chill for 24 hours.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Chilling improves flavour, controls spreading and creates thicker bakery-style cookies.

STEP 5 – Stuff Cookies

  • Divide chilled dough into 12 equal portions of approximately 90–95g each.
  • Flatten each portion slightly.
  • Place one frozen lava centre into the middle of each dough portion.
  • Wrap the dough around the filling and seal completely.
  • Roll into tall dough balls.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Tall dough balls create thicker cookies with gooey centres.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: If any filling is exposed, patch with extra dough before baking.

STEP 6 – Bake

  • Preheat oven to 180°C Fan OR 190°C Conventional.
  • Line baking trays with baking paper.
  • Place cookies well spaced apart on the trays.
  • Bake for 12–14 minutes.
  • If baking frozen stuffed dough balls, bake directly from frozen at 170°C Fan OR 180°C Conventional for approximately 14–16 minutes; no thawing is required.
  • Cookies should have golden edges, pale tops and slightly underbaked centres.
  • Allow cookies to cool on the tray for 15–20 minutes.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Do not overbake; the centres continue cooking from residual heat while cooling.

STEP 7 – Decorate

  • If using pre-prepared drizzle, microwave at 800W on HIGH for approximately 10 seconds OR until thick, melted and gooey.
  • The drizzle should flow slowly from a spoon; do not overheat because it can become thin and watery.
  • Drizzle generously over cooled cookies.
  • Sprinkle with 40g Crushed Biscoff Biscuits and an optional tiny pinch of flaky sea salt.
  • Allow drizzle to set for 5–10 minutes before serving.

NOTES

Reheating & Serving

  • Freshly Baked: Serve after cooling for 15–20 minutes; the centre should be soft, thick, gooey and similar to warm Biscoff cheesecake.
  • Day 2 Cookies: Microwave 8–10 seconds at full power OR 10–15 seconds at 50–70% power, then rest 1 minute.
  • Refrigerated Cookies: Microwave 12–15 seconds, then rest 1 minute.
  • Frozen Cookies – Best Method: Thaw overnight, then microwave 8–10 seconds.
  • Frozen Cookies – Microwave Direct: Microwave 30 seconds at 30–50% power, then 10 seconds at full power; rest 1 minute before serving.
  • Frozen Cookies – Oven Method: Bake from frozen at 170°C Fan OR 180°C Conventional for 14–16 minutes.

Professional Bakery Serving Tip

Microwave a cookie for 8–10 seconds, allow it to rest for 1 minute, then finish with an extra sprinkle of crushed Biscoff biscuits and a tiny pinch of flaky sea salt. The centre should slowly ooze like thick Biscoff cheesecake lava.

Storage

  • Room Temperature: Up to 3 days.
  • Refrigerator: Up to 5 days.
  • Freeze Unbaked Stuffed Dough Balls: Up to 3 months.
  • Bake From Frozen: Add 2–3 extra minutes or use the frozen oven method above.
  • Store decorated cookies in an airtight container.

Brown Sugar Tip For Serbia

Many Serbian brown sugars are drier than American packed brown sugar. For every 160g Brown Sugar, mix with 1 tsp Honey OR 1 tsp Molasses (Melasa), then rub together until evenly moistened. This helps replicate soft American-style brown sugar and improves chewiness.

Troubleshooting

  • Centre not molten? Filling was not frozen enough before baking.
  • Cookies spread too much? Dough was not chilled long enough.
  • Filling leaked? Dough was not sealed completely.
  • Cookies dry? They were overbaked.
  • Centres too firm? Microwave for 8–10 seconds before serving.
