ULTIMATE BAKERY-STYLE LOADED MUNCHMALLOW COOKIE

A thick bakery-style light chocolate malt cookie made with brown butter, toasted milk solids and crushed Munchmallow biscuit crumbs, wrapped around a whole frozen chocolate-coated marshmallow centre. Finished with a silky milk chocolate coating and crushed biscuit crumbs.

Preparation: Approx. 1 hour | Cooking: 12–14 minutes | Chilling: Overnight recommended | Total: Approx. 24 hours | Yield: 12 large cookies

SERVING TIMELINES

Fastest Serve Method: If all advance preparation is complete, bake, dip and serve in approximately 35–40 minutes.

Same Day Method: Allow approximately 8 hours from start to serving.

Ultimate Flavour Method: Allow approximately 24 hours including overnight chilling.

ADVANCE PREPARATION STEPS

STEP A1 – Brown Butter 🕒🥶

Preparation Time: Approx. 10 minutes OR up to 7 days ahead.

  • Brown 250g butter until golden and nutty.
  • During the final 30–45 seconds, whisk in 15g milk powder and toast lightly.
  • Transfer immediately to a heatproof bowl and cool.

Storage: Refrigerate up to 7 days or freeze up to 3 months.

Before Use: Cut into 2cm cubes and use immediately.

STEP A2 – Prepare Filling 🕒❄️

Preparation Time: Approx. 15 minutes OR up to 48 hours ahead.

  • Separate the chocolate-marshmallow tops from the biscuit bases.
  • Freeze the tops overnight.
  • Crush the biscuit bases finely for the dough and garnish.

Storage: Keep tops frozen; keep crumbs airtight.

Before Use: Use the tops directly from the freezer.

STEP A3 – Stuffed Dough Balls 🕒❄️

Preparation Time: Approx. 20 minutes OR up to 3 months ahead.

  • Divide dough into 12 portions of 90–95g.
  • Flatten, add one frozen centre, wrap and seal.
  • Roll into tall dough balls.

Storage: Freeze firm, then store airtight up to 3 months.

Before Use: Bake directly from frozen if desired.

STEP A4 – Chocolate Coating 🕒🥶🔥

Preparation Time: Approx. 5 minutes OR up to 72 hours ahead.

  • Melt milk chocolate in 20-second microwave intervals.
  • Stir until smooth and allow to cool before storing.

Storage: Refrigerate airtight up to 72 hours.

Before Use: Reheat gently until glossy and thick; do not overheat.

METHOD

STEP 1 – Prepare Brown Butter

  • Use pre-prepared solidified brown butter, or cool freshly browned butter and refrigerate until solid.

STEP 2 – Make Wet Mixture

  • Beat brown butter, white sugar and brown sugar for 2–3 minutes.
  • Add egg, egg yolk and vanilla; mix until smooth.

STEP 3 – Add Dry Ingredients

  • Whisk flour, biscuit crumbs, cocoa, cornstarch, baking soda, baking powder, salt and espresso powder.
  • Mix into wet ingredients only until no dry streaks remain.
  • Fold through milk chocolate chunks.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Do not overmix; overmixing produces tougher cookies.

STEP 4 – Chill Dough

  • Cover and chill at least 4 hours, preferably overnight and ideally 24 hours.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Chilling controls spreading and improves flavour.

STEP 5 – Stuff Cookies

  • Divide dough into 12 portions.
  • Flatten each portion, add one frozen marshmallow top and seal completely.
  • Roll into tall dough balls.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Patch any exposed filling with extra dough.

STEP 6 – Bake

  • Preheat to 180°C fan OR 190°C conventional.
  • Bake 12–14 minutes.
  • From frozen: bake at 170°C fan OR 180°C conventional for 14–16 minutes.
  • Cool on tray 15–20 minutes.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Do not overbake; the centre continues softening as the cookie cools.

STEP 7 – Decorate

  • Dip the bottom half of each cooled cookie into melted milk chocolate.
  • Sprinkle with reserved biscuit crumbs and optional flaky salt.
  • Allow coating to set before serving.

NOTES

  • Milk Chocolate = Milka Alpine or Lindt Swiss Classic
  • Flaky Sea Salt = Kotányi Pyramid Sea Salt Flakes or Maldon

Brown Sugar Tip for Serbia

Mix 160g dry brown sugar with 1 tsp honey until evenly moistened.

Storage & Reheating

  • Room temperature: up to 3 days.
  • Refrigerator: up to 5 days.
  • Freeze stuffed unbaked dough balls: up to 3 months.
  • Reheat day-two cookies for 8–10 seconds in the microwave.

Troubleshooting

  • Marshmallow disappeared? The centre was not frozen long enough.
  • Cookies spread? Dough was not chilled sufficiently.
  • Filling leaked? Dough was not completely sealed.
  • Cookies dry? They were overbaked.

Estimated Nutrition

Approx. 620–650 kcal per cookie.
