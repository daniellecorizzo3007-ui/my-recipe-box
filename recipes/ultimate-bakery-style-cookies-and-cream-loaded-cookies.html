ULTIMATE BAKERY-STYLE COOKIES & CREAM LOADED COOKIES

A thick brown-butter cookies-and-cream cookie packed with Oreo wafer crumbs and chunks, toasted milk powder and subtle white chocolate, wrapped around a frozen Oreo cheesecake truffle centre made with real Oreo cream. Finished with melted white chocolate, Oreo crumbs and a cream-filled Oreo quarter.

SERVING TIMELINES

Ultimate Method: Approx. 24–25 hours. Prepare brown butter, centres and dough on Day 1; freeze/chill overnight; stuff, bake and decorate on Day 2.

Same-Day Method: Allow approximately 7–8 hours. Freeze centres until completely solid and chill dough for at least 4 hours.

Fastest Serve Method: If frozen centres and stuffed cookie dough balls are already prepared, bake, rest and decorate in approximately 35–45 minutes.

Best Result: Overnight centre freezing plus 12–24 hours dough resting gives the best flavour, thickness and texture.

ADVANCE PREPARATION STEPS

STEP A1 – Brown Butter & Toast Milk Powder 🕒🥶

  • Place 230g unsalted butter in a light-coloured saucepan over medium heat.
  • Once melted and foaming, add 20g milk powder.
  • Stir or whisk frequently until the butter smells nutty and the milk solids become golden-hazelnut brown.
  • Do not take the butter extremely dark; Oreo should remain the dominant flavour.
  • Immediately transfer everything, including all toasted milk solids, to a heatproof bowl.
  • Weigh the mixture. Target approximately 185g. If slightly under, add enough ordinary melted unsalted butter to reach 185g.
  • Cool until no longer hot before making the dough.

Storage: Refrigerate airtight for up to 7 days.

STEP A2 – Frozen Oreo Cheesecake Truffle Centres 🕒❄️

  • Twist apart enough Oreos to obtain 45g cream filling and 65g wafers for the centres. Keep cream and wafers separate.
  • Beat 180g softened full-fat cream cheese until smooth.
  • Add 45g Oreo cream filling, 1 tsp vanilla and a small pinch of fine salt; mix until smooth.
  • Gradually mix in 55g melted and slightly cooled white chocolate.
  • Fold through 65g crushed Oreo wafers. Leave some small biscuit pieces rather than reducing everything to powder.
  • Divide into 12 portions of approximately 28–30g each and shape into compact balls or thick discs.
  • Freeze until completely solid. Overnight is preferred.

Storage: Freeze airtight for up to 1 month.

💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Fully frozen centres are essential. This keeps the cheesecake centre distinct and creamy instead of allowing it to disappear into the dough.

STEP A3 – Stuffed Cookie Dough Balls 🕒❄️

  • After the dough has chilled, divide it into 12 equal portions of approximately 82–85g each.
  • Flatten one portion into a thick disc.
  • Place one completely frozen Oreo cheesecake centre in the middle.
  • Bring the dough around the centre and seal every seam completely.
  • Shape into a tall, thick dough ball rather than a flat ball.
  • Repeat with all cookies.
  • Refrigerate stuffed cookies for 30–60 minutes before baking, or freeze for later baking.

Approximate stuffed weight: 110–115g each before garnish.

Storage: Freeze firm on a lined tray, then transfer to an airtight container or freezer bag for up to 3 months.

💡 Professional Baker's Tip: If any filling is visible, patch the area with a little extra dough before chilling or freezing.

STEP A4 – Prepare Garnish Components 🕒

  • Crush 25–30g Oreo wafers into garnish crumbs.
  • Cut 3 whole cream-filled Oreos carefully into quarters.
  • Store both components airtight until required.
  • Melt the 90g white chocolate only when ready to decorate, or melt gently and rewarm before use.

METHOD

STEP 1 – Prepare Brown Butter (STEP A1)

  • Prepare the brown butter and toasted milk powder exactly as described in STEP A1.
  • Allow the finished 185g mixture to cool until warm rather than hot.

STEP 2 – Prepare & Freeze Centres (STEP A2)

  • Prepare the 12 Oreo cheesecake truffle centres exactly as described in STEP A2.
  • Freeze until completely solid, preferably overnight.

STEP 3 – Mix Wet Ingredients

  • Add 130g light brown sugar, 80g white sugar and 35g reserved Oreo cream filling to the cooled browned butter.
  • Whisk thoroughly until glossy and evenly combined.
  • Add 1 large egg, 1 extra egg yolk and 2 tsp vanilla extract.
  • Whisk until smooth, slightly thickened and glossy.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Add vanilla after the butter has finished browning. Heating vanilla during browning drives off much of its aroma.

STEP 4 – Mix Dry Ingredients

  • In a separate bowl whisk together 310g plain flour, 18g cornstarch, 1 tsp baking soda, ½ tsp baking powder, 1 tsp fine salt and ¼ tsp espresso powder.
  • Add 40g finely crushed Oreo wafer crumbs and whisk until evenly distributed.
  • The espresso should not be detectable as coffee; it only supports the cocoa flavour of the Oreo wafers.

STEP 5 – Make & Rest the Dough

  • Add the dry mixture to the wet mixture and fold until almost combined.
  • Add 70g rough Oreo wafer pieces and 60g chopped white chocolate.
  • Fold only until evenly distributed. Do not overmix.
  • The dough should be thick, soft, heavily speckled with Oreo and visibly chunky, but should not resemble a full chocolate-cookie dough.
  • Cover tightly and refrigerate for at least 4 hours; overnight is preferred.

STEP 6 – Stuff the Cookies (STEP A3)

  • Divide chilled dough into 12 equal portions of approximately 82–85g each.
  • Flatten each portion into a thick disc.
  • Place one completely frozen centre into the middle.
  • Wrap the dough completely around the centre and seal every seam.
  • Roll gently into tall, thick dough balls.
  • Chill the stuffed cookies for 30–60 minutes before baking.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Tall dough balls encourage a thicker bakery-style cookie and help protect the creamy centre.

STEP 7 – Bake

  • Preheat oven to 180°C Fan OR 190°C Conventional.
  • Line baking trays with baking paper.
  • Place cookies well spaced apart.
  • Bake chilled cookies for 12–15 minutes.
  • If baking directly from frozen, use 170°C Fan OR 180°C Conventional and allow approximately 15–18 minutes, checking from 15 minutes.
  • The edges should be set and the tops beginning to crack while the centres still look slightly underdone.
  • Immediately after baking, use a large round cookie cutter or bowl to gently circle each cookie for a neat bakery shape.
  • Allow cookies to rest on the tray for 15–20 minutes before moving or decorating.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Do not wait for the centres to look fully baked. Residual heat continues cooking the cookies while they rest.

STEP 8 – Decorate (STEP A4)

  • Melt 90g white chocolate gently until smooth.
  • Add a small irregular dollop or drizzle to each cookie.
  • Immediately sprinkle with Oreo wafer crumbs.
  • Press one quarter of a whole cream-filled Oreo slightly off-centre into each cookie.
  • If desired, add only a tiny pinch of flaky sea salt.
  • Allow the topping to soft-set before storing.

NOTES

Reheating & Serving

  • Freshly Baked: Rest 15–20 minutes. The centre should be soft, creamy and truffle-like rather than liquid.
  • Day 2: Microwave approximately 8–12 seconds, then rest 1 minute.
  • Refrigerated: Microwave approximately 12–15 seconds, then rest 1 minute.
  • Frozen Baked Cookie: Thaw first, then microwave approximately 10–15 seconds.
  • Best serving temperature: Slightly warm, so the centre is soft while the cookie still retains structure.

Storage

  • Because the cookies contain a cream-cheese centre, refrigerate baked cookies once fully cooled.
  • Refrigerator: approximately 4–5 days in an airtight container.
  • Frozen unbaked stuffed dough balls: up to 3 months.
  • Frozen Oreo cheesecake centres: up to 1 month.
  • For best texture, bring refrigerated cookies closer to room temperature or warm briefly before serving.

Ideal Finished Cookie

Thick and softly set outside, chewy through the middle, packed with unmistakable Oreo wafer flavour and small creamy white-chocolate pockets, with a soft Oreo cheesecake truffle centre that remains visibly distinct when the cookie is broken open.

Brown Sugar Tip for Serbia

If the brown sugar available is unusually dry, mix the 130g brown sugar with approximately ¾ tsp honey before using. Rub it together until evenly moistened.

Troubleshooting

  • Filling leaks: Centre was not completely frozen, dough layer was too thin, or a seam was not fully sealed.
  • Centre disappears into dough: Freeze centres more thoroughly and avoid overbaking.
  • Cookies spread too much: Dough or stuffed cookies were too warm before baking.
  • Cookies stay too thick: Let chilled dough sit for a few minutes before stuffing, or press the tall dough ball very slightly before baking.
  • Cookies taste too sweet: Keep the white chocolate quantity restrained and ensure most Oreo pieces added to the dough have had the cream removed.
  • Centre too firm: Warm the baked cookie briefly before serving; on the next batch the centre white chocolate can be reduced slightly.

Optional Black Cocoa Upgrade

When black cocoa becomes available, replace 5g of the 310g plain flour with 5g black cocoa. Do not add it on top of the flour amount. The goal is to deepen authentic Oreo-wafer flavour without creating a full chocolate dough.
