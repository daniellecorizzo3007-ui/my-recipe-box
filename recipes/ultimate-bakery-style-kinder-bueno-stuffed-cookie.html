ULTIMATE BAKERY-STYLE KINDER BUENO STUFFED COOKIES

Thick brown-butter bakery cookies loaded with Kinder Chocolate and white chocolate, with a choice of flowing white-hazelnut lava centres or stable creamy Kinder Bueno truffle centres.

Preparation: 45 minutes | Freeze: 2–3 hours minimum | Bake: 11–13 minutes | Yield: 12 large cookies | Half batch: 6 cookies
KEY:🕒 Can Be Prepared In Advance🥶 Refrigerate❄️ Freeze🔥 Reheat Before Use💡 Professional Baker's Tip

INGREDIENTS BY STEP

Lava Centre – 12

  • 240g White Hazelnut Chocolate Spread
  • 60g Kinder Bueno, crushed
  • Pinch Fine Sea Salt

Truffle Centre – 12

  • 200g Kinder Bueno, finely crushed
  • 120g Kinder Chocolate, melted
  • 60g Mascarpone
  • 35g White Chocolate, melted
  • 25g Milk Powder
  • 15g White Hazelnut Spread
  • Pinch Fine Sea Salt

Cookie Dough – 12

  • 250g Unsalted Butter, browned to approx. 200g
  • 120g Light Brown Sugar

SERVING TIMELINES

Best Timeline: Make and freeze centres the day before; bake and decorate on serving day.

Same Day Timeline: Freeze centres for 2–3 hours, make dough, stuff, chill 30–60 minutes and bake.

ADVANCE PREPARATION STEPS

STEP A1 – Centres 🕒❄️

Preparation Time: Approx. 15–20 minutes plus freezing; can be made up to 1 month ahead.

  • Prepare either lava or truffle centre.
  • Portion and freeze until completely solid.

Storage: Freeze airtight up to 1 month.

Before Use: Use directly from freezer.

STEP A2 – Brown Butter 🕒🥶

Preparation Time: Approx. 10 minutes; can be made up to 48 hours ahead.

  • Brown butter until nutty and golden.
  • Weigh to 200g for 12 cookies or 100g for 6; top up with cold butter if needed.
  • Cool until warm, not hot.

Storage: Refrigerate airtight.

Before Use: Bring to cool-soft consistency.

STEP A3 – Stuffed Dough Balls 🕒❄️

Preparation Time: Approx. 20 minutes; can be frozen up to 2 months.

  • Portion dough into 90–95g pieces.
  • Insert one frozen centre and seal completely.
  • Freeze 30–60 minutes before baking.

Storage: Freeze airtight up to 2 months.

Before Use: Bake chilled or frozen; add 1–2 minutes if frozen.

METHOD

STEP 1 – Prepare Lava Centre

  • Mix white hazelnut spread, crushed Kinder Bueno and salt.
  • Portion into 12 x 25g mounds and freeze 2–3 hours.

STEP 2 – Prepare Truffle Centre

  • Mix crushed Kinder Bueno, melted Kinder Chocolate, mascarpone, melted white chocolate, milk powder, hazelnut spread and salt.
  • Portion into 12 balls of 35–38g and freeze solid.

STEP 3 – Brown Butter

  • Brown 250g butter until golden and nutty.
  • Weigh to approximately 200g and cool.

STEP 4 – Make Dough

  • Mix cooled brown butter and sugars.
  • Add egg, yolk and vanilla.
  • Add dry ingredients and mix until soft dough forms.
  • Fold through Kinder Chocolate and white chocolate chunks.

STEP 5 – Stuff Cookies

  • Divide into 12 portions of 90–95g.
  • Add one frozen centre, seal completely and form tall balls.
  • Freeze 30–60 minutes.

STEP 6 – Bake

  • Preheat to 170°C fan OR 180°C conventional.
  • Bake 11–13 minutes.
  • Cool on tray 15–20 minutes.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Do not overbake.

STEP 7 – Decorate

  • Top with melted Kinder Chocolate and a Kinder Bueno piece.
  • Optional: dip the base into melted Kinder Chocolate and allow to set.

NOTES

Which Centre?

The lava centre is softer and more dramatic. The truffle centre is richer, more stable and better for gifting.

Storage

  • Room temperature: 2–3 days airtight.
  • Fridge: up to 5 days.
  • Baked cookies or stuffed dough: freeze up to 2 months.

Reheating

  • Microwave: 8–12 seconds.
  • Oven: 150°C for 4–6 minutes.

Troubleshooting

  • Spread too much? Dough or butter was too warm.
  • Filling leaked? The dough was not sealed or centre not frozen.
  • Lava not runny? Reheat 8–12 seconds.
  • Truffle grainy? Chocolate was too hot when mixed with mascarpone.
