ULTIMATE BAKERY-STYLE TWIX CARAMEL LAVA COOKIES

A thick bakery-style brown-butter cookie loaded with real Twix flavours: toasted milk powder, crushed Twix biscuit through the dough, molten milk-chocolate-and-caramel Twix pieces, and a frozen Twix caramel cream lava centre. Finished with a soft-set caramel cream drizzle, crushed Twix biscuit, a small piece of Twix and a tiny touch of flaky sea salt.

Preparation: 60–75 minutes active|Bake: 12–15 minutes|Chilling: Overnight recommended|Total: Approx. 24 hours|Yield: 12 large cookies|Half batch: 6

SERVING TIMELINES

Ultimate Method: Approx. 24 hours. Prepare centres and dough on Day 1; stuff, bake and decorate on Day 2.

Same-Day Method: Allow approximately 7–8 hours. Freeze centres at least 4 hours and chill dough at least 4 hours.

Best Result: Overnight centre freezing plus 12–24 hours dough rest.

ADVANCE PREPARATION STEPS

STEP A1 – Brown Butter & Toast Milk Powder 🕒🥶

  • Place 250g butter in a light-coloured saucepan over medium heat.
  • Once melted and foaming, add 15g milk powder.
  • Whisk continuously until the butter smells nutty and the milk solids become golden-hazelnut brown.
  • Immediately transfer everything to a heatproof bowl, retaining all toasted milk solids.
  • Cool completely before use.

Storage: Refrigerate airtight up to 7 days.

STEP A2 – Prepare Twix Components 🕒🥶

  • Chill or partially freeze Twix bars before separating and chopping.
  • Prepare 40g finely crushed biscuit for the dough and 30g slightly coarser crumbs for decoration.
  • Prepare 180g chocolate/caramel pieces for dough, 100g for centres and 12 small pieces for decoration.
  • Keep prepared components refrigerated until needed.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Use a mixture of small, medium and larger Twix chocolate/caramel pieces so some melt into the dough while others remain as obvious molten pockets.

STEP A3 – Twix Caramel Lava Centres 🕒❄️

  • Heat 70g white sugar over medium-low heat until melted and medium amber.
  • Remove briefly from heat and carefully add 15g butter.
  • Slowly stir in 70g warmed cream; return briefly to low heat if needed until smooth.
  • Remove from heat and add 70g milk chocolate plus a tiny pinch of salt.
  • Rest 30 seconds, then stir until silky.
  • Cool 5–10 minutes, then fold through 100g chopped Twix chocolate/caramel pieces.
  • Cool until thick enough to portion.
  • Make 12 centres of approximately 20–22g each and freeze until completely solid.

Best: Freeze overnight. Minimum: 4 hours.

STEP A4 – Soft-Set Caramel Drizzle 🕒🥶🔥

  • Caramelise 50g white sugar to medium amber.
  • Remove from heat and add 10g butter.
  • Slowly stir in 50g warmed cream.
  • Return briefly to low heat if required until smooth.
  • Pour immediately over 90g finely chopped white chocolate.
  • Rest 1 minute, stir until silky and add a tiny pinch of fine sea salt.
  • Cool before storing.

Storage: Refrigerate airtight for approximately 3–4 days.

Before Use: Warm very gently until thick but drizzleable.

METHOD

STEP 1 – Prepare Brown Butter

  • If prepared ahead, soften the browned butter until cool-room-temperature consistency.
  • It should be soft enough to mix but not warm or liquid.

STEP 2 – Add Sugars

  • Beat 223–225g prepared browned butter with 100g white sugar and 160g light brown sugar for approximately 1–2 minutes until glossy and evenly combined.

STEP 3 – Add Egg, Yolk & Vanilla

  • Add 1 large egg, 1 extra egg yolk and 15ml vanilla extract.
  • Whisk until smooth, glossy and emulsified.
💡 Do not add vanilla while the butter is actively browning; adding it here preserves the vanilla aroma.

STEP 4 – Add Dry Ingredients

  • Whisk together 300g plain flour, 40g finely crushed Twix biscuit, 20g cornstarch, ¾ tsp baking soda, ½ tsp baking powder, ¾ tsp fine sea salt and 1 tsp espresso powder.
  • Fold the dry mixture into the wet ingredients only until a few tiny flour streaks remain.
  • Fold through 180g prepared Twix chocolate/caramel pieces.
💡 The espresso should not taste like coffee; it is included only to deepen the milk-chocolate and caramel flavours.

STEP 5 – Chill Dough

  • Cover tightly and refrigerate at least 4 hours.
  • 12 hours is better; 24 hours is best.
  • Up to 48 hours is acceptable.

STEP 6 – Stuff & Freeze

  • Divide dough into 12 equal portions.
  • Flatten each into a thick disc and place one fully frozen Twix caramel centre in the middle.
  • Wrap dough completely around the centre and seal every seam.
  • Shape into a tall, rough bakery-style ball; do not flatten.
  • Freeze stuffed cookies for approximately 30–60 minutes before baking.
💡 If any filling is exposed or the dough looks thin, patch with extra dough before freezing.

STEP 7 – Bake

  • Preheat oven to 180°C Fan OR 190°C Conventional.
  • Line a heavy baking tray with baking paper.
  • Place cookies well apart.
  • Bake for approximately 12–15 minutes.
  • Cookies are ready when edges are golden and set, tops are lightly golden and centres still look slightly underdone.
  • Immediately after baking, lightly bang the tray once against the bench for a crinkled bakery surface.
  • Cool on the tray for approximately 15 minutes before moving.
💡 Do not overbake. Residual heat continues cooking the cookies while they cool.

STEP 8 – Decorate

  • Warm the caramel drizzle gently until it falls from a spoon in thick ribbons.
  • Drizzle thin ribbons over almost completely cooled cookies.
  • Immediately sprinkle with crushed Twix biscuit.
  • Add one small Twix piece slightly off-centre on each cookie.
  • Finish with 2–4 tiny flakes of flaky sea salt.
  • Allow 15–30 minutes for the topping to soft-set.

NOTES

Reheating & Serving

  • Freshly Baked: Rest 15–20 minutes before serving.
  • Day 2: Microwave 8–12 seconds, then rest 1 minute.
  • Refrigerated: Microwave 12–15 seconds, then rest 1 minute.
  • Frozen Baked Cookie: Thaw first, then microwave 10–15 seconds.

Storage

  • Room temperature: approximately 3 days airtight.
  • Refrigerator: approximately 5 days.
  • Frozen unbaked stuffed cookies: up to 3 months.
  • Frozen centres: approximately 1 month.

Ideal Finished Cookie

Golden, slightly crisp and crinkled outside; soft and chewy inside; molten Twix pockets throughout; and a thick Twix caramel cream centre that slowly oozes when warm.

Brown Sugar Tip for Serbia

If the brown sugar is unusually dry, mix 160g brown sugar with approximately 1 tsp honey before using.

Troubleshooting

  • Filling leaks: centre was not fully frozen, dough was too thin or seams were not sealed.
  • Centre not gooey: warm cookie briefly before serving.
  • Cookie spreads too much: stuffed dough was not cold enough.
  • Cookie is dry: it was overbaked.
  • Drizzle too soft: allow it to cool fully and avoid overheating.
  • Caramel centre hard: caramel was cooked too dark before adding cream.
