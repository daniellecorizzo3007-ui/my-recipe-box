ULTIMATE BAKERY-STYLE LOADED CARROT CAKE CHEESECAKE COOKIES

A thick bakery-style carrot cake cookie made with brown butter, toasted milk solids, bloomed spices, fresh orange zest and toasted walnuts, wrapped around a frozen vanilla cheesecake centre and finished with a light cream cheese drizzle.

Preparation: Approx. 1 hour|Bake: 12–15 minutes|Chilling: Overnight recommended|Total: Approx. 24 hours|Yield: 12 large cookies

SERVING TIMELINES

Fastest Serve Method: If the cheesecake centres, browned butter, toasted walnuts and stuffed dough balls have been prepared in advance, bake, cool, drizzle and serve in approximately 35–40 minutes.

Same Day Method: Allow approximately 8 hours from the start of preparation until serving, including freezing and dough chilling.

Ultimate Flavour Method: Allow approximately 24 hours, including an overnight dough rest.

ADVANCE PREPARATION STEPS

STEP A1 – Cheesecake Centres 🕒❄️

Preparation Time: Approx. 10 minutes OR can be prepared up to 1 month in advance.

  • Mix full-fat cream cheese, icing sugar, vanilla and a pinch of fine salt until smooth.
  • Divide into 12 equal portions on a baking-paper-lined tray.
  • Freeze for at least 2 hours or until completely solid.

Storage: Freeze in an airtight container for up to 1 month.

Before Use: Use directly from the freezer.

STEP A2 – Toast Walnuts 🕒

Preparation Time: Approx. 10 minutes OR can be prepared up to 7 days in advance.

  • Bake walnuts at 170°C for 8–10 minutes.
  • Cool completely, then chop into medium 5–8mm pieces.

Storage: Store airtight at room temperature for up to 7 days.

Before Use: Ensure walnuts are completely cool before adding to the dough.

STEP A3 – Brown Butter, Toast Milk Powder & Bloom Spices 🕒🥶

Preparation Time: Approx. 12 minutes OR can be prepared up to 7 days in advance.

  • Brown 250g unsalted butter until golden and nutty.
  • During the final 30–45 seconds, whisk in 15g full-cream milk powder.
  • Add cinnamon, nutmeg and ginger and stir for approximately 15 seconds to bloom the spices.
  • Immediately transfer to a heatproof bowl, including all toasted milk solids.
  • Target yield: approximately 223–225g.

Storage: Refrigerate airtight for up to 7 days or freeze up to 3 months.

Before Use: Re-solidify until soft and spreadable, then cut into approximately 2cm cubes.

STEP A4 – Stuffed Cookie Dough Balls 🕒❄️

Preparation Time: Approx. 20 minutes OR can be prepared up to 2 months in advance.

  • Portion chilled dough into 12 pieces of approximately 90–100g.
  • Flatten each portion, add one frozen cheesecake centre and seal completely.
  • Shape into tall dough balls.

Storage: Freeze until firm, then store airtight for up to 2 months.

Before Use: Bake chilled or directly from frozen using the instructions below.

METHOD

STEP 1 – Prepare Cheesecake Centres

  • Mix 200g full-fat cream cheese, 60g icing sugar, 1 tsp vanilla and a pinch of fine salt until smooth.
  • Divide into 12 portions and freeze until completely solid.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Frozen filling is essential for keeping the centre creamy instead of disappearing into the dough.

STEP 2 – Toast Walnuts

  • Bake 100g walnuts at 170°C for 8–10 minutes.
  • Cool completely and chop into 5–8mm pieces.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Toasting strengthens flavour, improves texture and adds bakery-style depth.

STEP 3 – Brown Butter, Toast Milk Powder & Bloom Spices

  • Brown 250g unsalted butter until golden and nutty.
  • Whisk in 15g milk powder during the final 30–45 seconds.
  • Add cinnamon, nutmeg and ginger; stir for approximately 15 seconds.
  • Transfer immediately to a heatproof bowl.
  • Weigh the mixture; target approximately 223–225g. If short, add 1–2 tsp milk.
  • Cool and refrigerate until soft and spreadable.

STEP 4 – Prepare Carrots

  • Finely grate 180g carrots.
  • Place in clean paper towel or cheesecloth and squeeze gently to remove only excess water.
  • Do not squeeze completely dry.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Excess carrot moisture can cause flat cookies, but removing too much moisture can make them dry.

STEP 5 – Make Cookie Dough

  • Beat 223–225g re-solidified brown butter with 120g white sugar and 150g packed light brown sugar for 2–3 minutes.
  • If the Serbian brown sugar is dry, rub it with 1 tsp honey before using.
  • Add 1 large egg, 1 egg yolk and 2 tsp vanilla; mix until smooth.
  • Whisk together 325g flour, 20g cornstarch, 1 tsp baking soda, ½ tsp baking powder, 1 tsp fine salt and ½ tsp espresso powder.
  • Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and mix only until nearly combined.
  • Fold in the prepared carrots, orange zest and toasted walnuts.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Do not overmix. The dough should be thick and soft.

STEP 6 – Chill Dough

  • Cover and refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours.
  • For best flavour and texture, chill overnight.
  • For the ultimate result, chill for 24 hours.

STEP 7 – Stuff Cookies

  • Divide dough into 12 equal portions of approximately 90–100g each.
  • Flatten each portion and place one frozen cheesecake centre in the middle.
  • Seal completely and shape into tall dough balls.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Patch any exposed filling with extra dough before baking.

STEP 8 – Bake

  • Preheat oven to 180°C fan OR 190°C conventional.
  • Bake chilled cookies for 12–15 minutes.
  • From frozen, bake at 170°C fan OR 180°C conventional for approximately 14–17 minutes.
  • The edges should be lightly golden and the centres soft.
  • Cool on the tray for 15–20 minutes.
💡 Professional Baker's Tip: Do not overbake; residual heat continues cooking the centres.

STEP 9 – Cream Cheese Drizzle

  • Mix 60g icing sugar, 15g cream cheese, 1–2 tsp milk and ¼ tsp vanilla until smooth and pipeable.
  • Drizzle lightly over fully cooled cookies.
  • Finish with toasted walnut crumbs and a very small amount of fresh orange zest.

NOTES

Reheating & Serving

  • Freshly baked: cool for 15–20 minutes before serving.
  • Day 2: microwave for approximately 8–10 seconds, then rest 1 minute.
  • Refrigerated: microwave for approximately 12–15 seconds, then rest 1 minute.
  • Frozen baked cookies: thaw overnight before gently warming.

Professional Bakery Serving Tip

Serve slightly warm so the carrot cake cookie is soft and the cheesecake centre becomes creamy. Add the drizzle only after the cookie has cooled enough that it will not melt.

Storage

  • Because of the cream cheese centre, refrigerate decorated cookies in an airtight container.
  • Refrigerator: up to 4–5 days.
  • Freeze baked cookies: up to 2 months.
  • Freeze unbaked stuffed dough balls: up to 2 months.

Brown Sugar Tip for Serbia

For every 150g dry Serbian brown sugar, mix with approximately 1 tsp honey and rub together until evenly moistened.

Troubleshooting

  • Cookies spread too much? Carrots were too wet, dough was too warm, or dough was not chilled long enough.
  • Cookies dry? Too much moisture was removed from the carrots or the cookies were overbaked.
  • Filling leaked? Dough was not completely sealed or filling was not frozen solid.
  • Centre not creamy? Gently reheat for 8–10 seconds before serving.
